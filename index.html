Walkable Area
Click on the canvas to add vertices. Click Finish to close the polygon. Click a vertex to select it, drag to move it, or delete it with the button below.
Hotspots
Editing Hotspot: Select a hotspot and click on the canvas to add vertices. Click Finish to close.
Vertices: Click a vertex to select it, drag to move it. Click an edge to add a new vertex.
Keys: Delete/Backspace to remove the selected vertex, Escape to deselect.
Character Scaling
Tip: Draw two horizontal lines on the canvas to define scaling planes. Drag the lines to move them. The top line (background) and bottom line (foreground) set character scale based on Y position.
